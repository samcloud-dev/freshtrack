Not all dates mean the same thing

FreshTrack keeps the two common label dates apart, so you know which one you're looking at.

Best before

About quality: taste, texture, freshness. FreshTrack shows it so you can decide what to use first.

Expiration date

A firmer date found on some products. FreshTrack shows it separately, so you never mix the two up. Follow the guidance on the package.
